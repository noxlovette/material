## Standard or connected

- **`ButtonGroup`** (standard) holds separate actions, such as Back, Play, Next. Pressing a
  button widens it by 15% while the buttons beside it narrow to make room, then all settle back.
- **`ConnectedButtonGroup`** holds related options, one or several selected, such as Walk, Bike,
  Drive. It replaces the segmented button.

## Contents

Mix `Button`, `Toggle` and `ButtonIcon` freely, in the filled, tonal, outlined or elevated
styles. Avoid text buttons and standard icon buttons, which have no container to change. A
toggle inside swaps its shape as it's selected.

## Size

Set `size` to the buttons' size. It sets the space between them so each keeps a 48dp target:
18dp at XS, 12dp at S, 8dp from M up. The group takes its buttons' height.

<Canvas of={GroupStories.Sizes} />

The press interaction is horizontal only, and off under reduced motion.
