## Anatomy

- **Leading button:** the main action, with a label, an icon, or both. An icon-only leading
  button needs an `aria-label`.
- **Trailing button:** always a menu icon. It opens the menu (`menuLabel` names it) and flips the
  icon while open.

The two share one colour style (elevated, filled, tonal or outlined; there is no text split
button), and opening the menu doesn't change their colours.

## Shape and size

The buttons sit 2dp apart. Their outer corners are fully round; the inner corners are small at
rest and round out while hovered, focused or pressed. While the menu is open, the trailing
button becomes fully round and centres its icon, which sits slightly toward the leading button
otherwise.

| `size` | Height | Inner corner | Hovered/pressed | Trailing width | Menu icon |
| ------ | ------ | ------------ | --------------- | -------------- | --------- |
| `xs`   | 32dp   | 4dp          | 8dp             | 48dp           | 22dp      |
| `sm`   | 40dp   | 4dp          | 12dp            | 48dp           | 22dp      |
| `md`   | 56dp   | 4dp          | 12dp            | 56dp           | 26dp      |
| `lg`   | 96dp   | 8dp          | 20dp            | 96dp           | 38dp      |
| `xl`   | 136dp  | 12dp         | 20dp            | 136dp          | 50dp      |
