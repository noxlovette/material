## Sizes

| `size`    | FAB  | Icon | Corners | Extended: label, padding, gap |
| --------- | ---- | ---- | ------- | ----------------------------- |
| `regular` | 56dp | 24dp | 16dp    | title medium, 16dp, 8dp       |
| `medium`  | 80dp | 28dp | 20dp    | title large, 26dp, 12dp       |
| `large`   | 96dp | 36dp | 28dp    | headline small, 28dp, 16dp    |
| `small`   | 40dp | 24dp | 12dp    | none                          |

`small` is the baseline small FAB, which M3 no longer recommends. FABs sit at elevation 3 and
rise to 4 on hover. Keep 16dp from the window edges.

## Colour

`primary-container` (default), `secondary-container` and `tertiary-container`, or the stronger
`primary`, `secondary` and `tertiary`. The state layer always takes the icon's colour.

## FAB and extended FAB

Give the FAB a `label` and it becomes an extended FAB where there's room. The two transform
into each other with the space available, so `expanded` is responsive:

- **Default:** a FAB on compact windows, an extended FAB from medium windows up
  (`{ small: false, medium: true }`).
- **In a `Rail`:** a FAB while the rail is collapsed, an extended FAB while it's expanded.
- **Your own:** pass `true`, `false`, or per-window values; toggle it to collapse the label
  while the page scrolls.

The label slides open and shut on the spatial spring. It also names the FAB for assistive
technology when collapsed; a FAB without a label needs an `aria-label`.

## FAB menu

`withMenu` opens up to six `FABMenuItem`s above the FAB, which springs into a 56dp round close
button in the same top trailing corner. The close button is 56dp whatever the FAB's size, as
M3 specifies, so a medium or large FAB shrinks into it. The items grow out of it one after
another, nearest first. Items are 56dp pills 4dp apart, 8dp above the close
button, in the FAB's colour set: a primary, secondary or tertiary close button with items in the
matching container colour. The menu takes arrow keys, Escape and outside clicks.

## Container transform

Pass a `surface` snippet and the FAB opens into it: the FAB's container grows from its corner
into the surface and turns from the FAB's colour into the surface's, and the content fades in
once it has room (M3's container transform, with a persistent container). It shrinks back on
Escape, an outside click, or the snippet's `close()`, and focus returns to the FAB. Keep the
FAB's icon in the surface, as on the "Compose new" row below, so the icon persists too.

## Migrating

`config` is now `color`, with the Expressive names: the old `primary` is now `primary-container`
(the default). `size` gains `medium`. `FABMenuItem` loses `variant`; items follow the FAB's
colour. `expanded` now defaults to responsive instead of `false`, so a labelled FAB extends on
wider windows; pass `expanded={false}` to keep the old behaviour.
