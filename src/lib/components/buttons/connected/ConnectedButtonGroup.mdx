## When to use

For a small set of related options that change what the page shows, such as Walk, Bike, Drive.
It replaces M3's segmented button. For separate actions, use `ButtonGroup`.
`type="single"` keeps one item selected; `type="multiple"` allows several.

## Colour and shape

Items are toggle buttons in the group's `variant`: filled (default), tonal, outlined or
elevated, with the same unselected and selected colours as `Toggle`. Selection shows through
colour and shape, not a check mark:

- Items sit 2dp apart. The outer corners are fully round, or the inner size with
  `shape="square"`.
- Inner corners are 8dp (16dp at L, 20dp at XL), tighten while pressed, and round out fully on
  the selected item. Neighbours never change.

## Accessibility

Name the group with `aria-label`, and give icon-only items an `aria-label`. Arrow keys move
between items; Space and Enter select. XS and S items are at least 48dp wide.
