## Modal or standard

- **`modal`** (default) opens at the window's end edge above a scrim, and blocks the app until
  it's closed with its close button, Escape, or a scrim tap. Use it on compact and medium windows,
  or when the content needs full attention. It is a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`,
  so the browser handles the focus trap, top-layer stacking and making the page behind it inert.
- **`standard`** is part of the layout. Make it the last child of a full-height row. It opens by
  widening, so the content beside it reflows instead of being covered, and everything stays
  usable. Use it on expanded windows, for filters or details that people use alongside the main
  content.

```svelte
<div class="flex h-dvh">
  <main class="min-w-0 flex-1">…</main>
  <SideSheet variant="standard" headline="Filters" bind:open>…</SideSheet>
</div>
```

## Anatomy

- **Header:** an optional back button (`onback`), the `headline`, and a close button. The headline
  is `title-large` and names the sheet for assistive technology.
- **Content:** your `children`, padded 24dp. It scrolls inside the sheet.
- **Actions** (optional): the `actions` snippet, pinned to the bottom and left-aligned below a
  divider. Put the most important action first, e.g. Save before Cancel.

Show `onback` only when the sheet navigates within itself. The header's start padding then drops
from 24dp to 16dp.

## Size and shape

Side sheets are 256dp wide by default and never wider than 400dp, and they span the full height.

|          | Docked                                                               | `detached`                        |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Modal    | `surface-container-low`, elevation 1, 16dp corners on the inner side | 16dp from the edges, 16dp corners |
| Standard | `surface`, elevation 0, square, divider on the inner edge            | 16dp from the edges, 16dp corners |

`divider={false}` removes the edge divider and the one above the actions.

<Canvas of={SheetStories.StandardDetached} />

## Motion

A modal sheet slides in from the edge on the spatial spring, without fading, and stays mounted
until it has slid out. A standard sheet animates its width on the same spring, so the content
beside it reflows as it slides.

## Not yet supported

Right-to-left layouts: the modal sheet sits at the end edge, but always slides in from the right.
