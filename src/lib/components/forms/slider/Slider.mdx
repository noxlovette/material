This is the M3 Expressive slider. It has two tracks, active and inactive, with a 6dp gap on each
side of a 4dp bar handle. Bind `value` to read and set it:

```svelte
<script lang="ts">
  let volume = $state(40);
</script>

<Slider bind:value={volume} min={0} max={100} />
```

## Choosing a size

`xs` is the default. Go larger when the slider is the main control on the screen, or when it needs
inset icons.

| Size | Track | Handle | Outer corner | Inset icon   |
| ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------ | ------------ |
| `xs` | 16dp  | 44dp   | 8dp          | —            |
| `s`  | 24dp  | 44dp   | 8dp          | —            |
| `m`  | 40dp  | 52dp   | 12dp         | 24dp, 6dp in |
| `l`  | 56dp  | 68dp   | 16dp         | 24dp, 6dp in |
| `xl` | 96dp  | 108dp  | 28dp         | 32dp, 8dp in |

The corners next to the handle are always 2dp.

## Inset icons

`leadingIconProps` and `trailingIconProps` put an icon inside the track. M3 defines inset icons
only for `m`, `l` and `xl`, so they're ignored on `xs` and `s`. When the handle gets too close for
the icon to fit in its track, the icon moves across the handle into the other track. It also takes
that track's color.

## Continuous or discrete

Leave `step="any"` for a continuous slider. Set a numeric `step` to make it discrete, and add
`stops` to show a 4dp stop indicator at each step. With or without `stops`, `endStops` marks the
end of the track 4dp from its edge. Stops hide within 6dp of the handle, and wherever an inset
icon covers them.

## Value indicator

With `showValue`, a 48×44dp indicator shows above the handle while it's dragged or has keyboard
focus. It sits 12dp above the handle, on inverse-surface. Use `format` to add units or localize
the number.

## States

- **Pressed and focused:** the handle narrows from 4dp to 2dp on a fast spatial spring, and the
  6dp gaps follow its edge.
- **Disabled:** the active track is on-surface at 38% opacity, the inactive track 12%, and the
  handle 38%.

## Accessibility

- **Keyboard:** the arrow keys move by one step, and Home/End jump to `min`/`max` (via bits-ui's
  `Slider.Root`).
- **Focus:** keyboard focus narrows the handle, draws an outline around it, and reveals the value
  indicator.
- **Labelling:** give the slider an accessible name, such as `aria-label="Volume"`. Inset icons
  are decorative and don't label it.
