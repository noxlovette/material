## Outlined or filled

`variant` defaults to `outlined`: a bordered field whose floating label opens a gap in the border.
`filled` has a container and a bottom indicator line, and carries more emphasis. Use outlined in
busy surroundings (over an image, in dense forms) or next to a filled field that should stand
out.

<Canvas of={TextfieldStories.Outlined} />

The `label` starts inside the field and floats up on focus or once there's a value.

```svelte
<Textfield label="Email" bind:value={email} required>
  {#snippet supportingText()}
    We'll never share your email.
  {/snippet}
</Textfield>
```

## Icons and supporting text

`leadingIconProps` and `trailingIconProps` add icons (a trailing icon is a good place for a clear
button). The `supportingText` snippet renders helper or error text below the field. Other native
`<input>` attributes (`type`, `maxlength`, `autocomplete`…) pass through to the input.

## Accessibility

- **Label.** The floating label is a real `<label for>` tied to the input's id (generated when you
  don't pass one), not an `aria-label`.
- **Errors.** `error` sets `aria-invalid`. Say what's wrong in `supportingText`, not just with
  color.
- **Required.** `required` sets `required` and `aria-required`, and shows an asterisk that screen
  readers skip.
